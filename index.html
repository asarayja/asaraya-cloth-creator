Asarayja Cloth Creator

A Blender add-on that gets clothing, jewellery and props ready for GTA V and FiveM.

It handles the parts of the job that otherwise require knowing the format by heart: weights that keep a garment on the body, jiggle that follows movement, the right file name for the clothing slot, the right shader, and the prop format for earrings and bracelets.

First things first

If you have not installed the add-on yet, start with Installation. If you are unsure about the order of things, read How it works — that page shows the whole flow on one screen.

One rule that solves most problems

Build a Base Body first. Almost everything in the add-on measures against the body: weights are copied from it, jiggle comes from its skeleton, poke-through is measured against its skin. With no body in the scene, most buttons will simply tell you one is missing.

See Base Body.
